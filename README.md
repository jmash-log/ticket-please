# Ticket Edu Money!

*A game of rules, rails & royal nonsense.*

The Kingdom has one train, the Royal Railway Authority has a rule for everything, and you've just been hired to enforce them.

**Ticket Edu Money!** is a comedic, narrative-driven browser game where you play as a junior ticket inspector at Karukutty Central. Inspect passengers, examine their tickets, navigate increasingly complicated regulations, and decide who gets to board.

Sounds simple enough. Until royalty gets involved.

## Gameplay

Every passenger brings a new situation to evaluate. Examine their ticket, consult the regulations currently in effect, and make your decision.

As your shift progresses, new decrees introduce additional complications. Rules accumulate, decisions become trickier, and you'll need to pay attention to more than just the obvious details.

And when the King himself arrives at the station, you'll face a decision that could define your entire shift.

## Your Shift

- **Clock in:** Meet Rukku, the station announcer, and discover your new workplace.
- **Learn the ropes:** Get introduced to the railway's regulations before inspections begin.
- **Inspect passengers:** Examine tickets, evaluate the details, and make your calls.
- **Adapt to new decrees:** Keep up as the requirements evolve throughout your shift.
- **Face the King:** Make your final decision and discover how your story ends.

## Decisions & Scoring

Your decisions are evaluated against the rules in effect at the time of each inspection.

The game tracks your performance, records incorrect decisions, and calculates your inspection accuracy. At the end of the shift, you'll receive a report summarising your performance and final ruling.

Your choices at the climax also determine which of the game's three endings you experience.

## Built With

- **HTML5** — Game structure and screens
- **CSS3** — Visual design, animations, and responsive layouts
- **JavaScript** — Game logic, rule validation, scoring, decision tracking, and branching endings

## Getting Started

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in a modern web browser.
3. Start your shift at Karukutty Central.

Alternatively, run a local server from the project directory:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then open `http://localhost:8000` in your browser.

No build step or backend is required.
